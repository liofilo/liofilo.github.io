01 — The island

From the green heart
of Corfu.

On Corfu, olive trees rise improbably tall—silver leaves above stone terraces, carried by the Ionian light. This is where Liofilo begins.

Our fruit is gathered at the first blush of the season, while it is still vividly green and alive with possibility.

39° 37′ N  ·  19° 55′ E   /   IONIAN SEA

02 — The fruit

Lianolia
Kerkyras.

The olive native to Corfu: small, elongated, and rich with oil. In the press, it gives a robust fruitiness, a graceful bitterness, and aromas that recall cherry blossom and cinnamon wood.

03 — Bio · Early harvest

Picked before
the fruit softens.

Grown organically, then harvested early while the olives are still firm and green. It means less oil, but more of what makes it singular: fragrance, structure, and naturally occurring polyphenols.

02

Natural defence

Polyphenols are naturally occurring olive antioxidants. When independently verified at qualifying levels, they contribute to the protection of blood lipids from oxidative stress.

03

Measured, not assumed

Each harvest will be lab-tested before a specific polyphenol claim is made. The EU-authorised benefit applies with 20 g daily from qualifying oils.
